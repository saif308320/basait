01 — The Origin

Grown high.
Picked by hand.

We buy washed and natural lots from farms we have stood on. Elevation, cultivar, and the farmer’s name sit on every bag — never a vague “blend of origins.”

  • Yirgacheffe, Ethiopia1,900–2,200 m · heirloom · jasmine, bergamot, white peach
  • Huila, ColombiaFinca El Paraíso · Caturra & Castillo · caramel, red apple, cocoa
  • Nyeri, KenyaSL28 / SL34 · washed · blackcurrant, grapefruit, brown sugar

01b — Harvest window

We buy in season,
not from a warehouse.

Ethiopia lands October through January. Colombia twice a year. Kenya in the main crop. Bags rest 10–14 days after roast before they leave Portland.

02 — The Roast

Heat as a
craft tool.

Every lot is profiled on a 15kg Probat. First crack is a decision, not a finish line. We roast for sweetness and structure — darkness is never the default.

02b — Quality lab

Cupped twice.
Shipped once.

Green is sampled on arrival. Roast is cupped 24 hours later against the previous lot. If the table does not match, the batch does not leave the warehouse.

04 — Brew Guide

Three ways
to the cup.

  1. Rinse a V60. 20g coffee, medium-fine.
  2. Bloom 50g water at 96°C for 30 seconds.
  3. Pulse to 150g by 1:00, then 320g by 2:15.
  4. Finish draining around 2:45–3:00.
  1. 18g in, 36g out, 26–30 seconds.
  2. Stream should look like warm honey.
  3. Dawn Patrol at 1:2. Highland Ghost at 1:2.2.
  4. Purge the group between shots.
  1. 30g coarse to 500g water at 94°C.
  2. Stir at 0:30, steep four minutes.
  3. Skim crust, plunge slowly, decant.
  4. Best with Warehouse Dark and House.

Before you ask

Shipping, grind, freshness.

When do you roast my bag?

Subscription bags roast Monday and Thursday. One-time orders ship within two roast days of payment.

Do you ship whole bean only?

No. We grind for espresso, pour-over, and press if you ask. Whole bean still tastes better after day three.

Can I visit without a tour?

Yes. The cafe is walk-in. The roastery floor is tours and wholesale by appointment.

Do you take wholesale outside Oregon?

Washington yes. Beyond that we talk if you already have a service relationship nearby.
